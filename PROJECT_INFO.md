# Cursed Mystery Box

Players choose an exact Cursed Tier, Random Tier, or No Cap mode, optionally exclude discovered relics they have not unlocked, and spin an animated Mystery Box to receive a map and relic challenge.

Exact and random tier draws match their cursed-points target. No Cap selects any number of eligible relics and may exceed Tier III. Round-Based and Survival maps rotate through a shuffled bag without repeats until every map has appeared.

Relics marked as undiscovered in the app data are automatically filtered out of every draw.
